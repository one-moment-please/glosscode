# GlossCode logo

A glossy navy rounded square holding a striped letter. The white stripes are cut from a **G** and the amber stripes from a **C**, the same way GlossCode alternates a line of G-code with a line of English. The C has a wider mouth and no crossbar, so the opening of the letter looks like a comb where the two shapes differ.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `glosscode.svg` | Master artwork, 9 stripes. Use at 64 px and larger. |
| `glosscode-small.svg` | Simplified 5 stripes with a heavier stroke. Use at 48 px and smaller. |
| `glosscode.ico` | Windows app icon: 16, 24, 32 and 48 from the small artwork; 64, 128 and 256 from the master. |
| `glosscode-*.png` | Transparent PNG exports at 16, 32, 48, 128, 256 and 512 px. |

Colors: background `#1d4f8f` to `#081a36`; G stripes `#ffffff` to `#cfe0f5`; C stripes `#ffd27a` to `#f08a1c`.
